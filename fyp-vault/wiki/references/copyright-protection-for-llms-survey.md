# Copyright Protection for Large Language Models Survey

"Copyright Protection for Large Language Models: A Survey of Methods, Challenges, and Trends" surveys technologies for protecting LLMs, with emphasis on [[concepts/llm-fingerprinting|model fingerprinting]].

## Key Claims

- The survey argues that prior surveys focused heavily on tracing LLM-generated text through text watermarking.
- It clarifies relationships among text watermarking, model watermarking, and model fingerprinting.
- It categorizes model fingerprinting approaches for LLM copyright protection.
- It discusses fingerprint transfer and [[concepts/fingerprint-removal-and-adversarial-robustness|fingerprint removal]].
- It summarizes metrics including effectiveness, harmlessness, robustness, stealthiness, and reliability.

## Project Relevance

This survey is the terminology backbone for [[concepts/llm-copyright-protection|LLM copyright protection]] and helps separate model-level fingerprints from text-level watermarks.
